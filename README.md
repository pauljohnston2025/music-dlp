# MusicDLP

## Why this project is needed?

Downloading full playlists often results in random songs that do not match preferences, and also results in duplicates sometimes. This app is designed to solve these issues by providing a quick and intuitive interface (like Tinder) to go through a playlist and decide if you like a song or hate it. 

### Key Benefits:
- **Quality Control**: Decide song-by-song whether to download or skip.
- **Efficiency**: Faster than manually copying links of individual songs you like.
- **Organization**: Keep track of liked and disliked songs to avoid seeing them again.
- **Offline Access**: Liked songs are saved to disk for use in any music player or for manual export.

## Features:
- **Tinder-like Swiping**: Swipe right to like and download, swipe left to dislike and skip forever.
- **Fast Previews**: Stream and seek through songs to quickly decide.
- **Automated Filtering**: Already liked/disliked songs are hidden from future views.
- **Disk Saving**: Songs are downloaded to standard music folders on your device.
- **Searchable History**: Easily find and play songs you've already swiped.
